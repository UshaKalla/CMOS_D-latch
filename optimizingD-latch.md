Problem! It is structurally impossible to create an AND or OR gate with NMOS and PMOS. Therefore, to make an AND gate, we need 2 NAND gates and a NOR gate. 

NAND Gate 
2 PMOS + 2 NMOS
NOR Gate 
1 PMOS + 1 NMOS 

 = 6 Transistors to make one AND gate 

 we only need 4 transistors to make CMOS NOR gate!
